# NIGOH Family — Master Architecture, Sitemap & Screen Inventory

## 1. Project Overview & Guiding Philosophy
**NIGOH Family** is an ethical, privacy-first parental control and digital wellbeing platform designed to foster trust, transparency, and balanced digital habits between parents and children.
- **Core Ethical Tenet:** Visible consent, zero covert surveillance, transparent rule enforcement, offline resilience, and empowering child autonomy with respectful communication.
- **Platform Matrix:** Responsive Website, Ubuntu 26.04 (GNOME), Windows 11 (Fluent), macOS (Apple Human Interface), Android Parent & Child (Material 3), and iPhone Parent & Child (Bento UI & Floating Glass Bar).
