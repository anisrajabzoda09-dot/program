Features

Everything you need in one app

One app for both phones. When you first open it, you choose a role: parent or child.

Location on a map

Your child's location updates automatically, and you can see when it was last updated.

App list

Every installed app, with its icon and minutes used today.

Block with one switch

Block or unblock any app. The rule takes effect on your child's phone right away.

Daily limits

From 15 minutes to 4 hours a day, set separately for each app.

Family chat

Messages between parent and child, plus an SOS button.

Parent PIN

Without the PIN, your child can't turn off protection.
App rules

Screen time with clear rules

Set a rule for each app. When a limit runs out or an app is blocked, the NIGOH screen covers it.

  • Daily limit: 15 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours, 2 hours, 4 hours or no limit.
  • “Homework time”: the app is blocked on chosen days and hours, for example Mon–Fri, 16:00–18:00.
  • “Pause”: all apps are blocked at once, for example during dinner.
  • Rules work offline too and sync automatically when the phone reconnects.

Location and chat

Know where your child is

Your child's location is shown on an OpenStreetMap map and updates automatically. If you need to, send a message in the family chat. There's also a 24-hour history and safe places.

  • Location with the time of the last update.
  • Messages with “Read” receipts and quick replies.
  • An SOS button that shares location and battery level.
Setup

Get started in three steps

Install the app on both phones

One APK for parent and child. Choose the role when you open it.

Link the phones

The child's phone shows a QR code or a 6-digit code. The parent scans it. It takes less than a minute.

Set the rules

Choose limits, “Homework time” and which apps to block.
