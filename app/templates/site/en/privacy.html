Privacy Policy

We explain in plain language what data NIGOH Family collects, why, and how you can manage it.

Published: October 4, 2026 For: the Android app and nigohfamily.qobus.tj website

In brief

  • Data is collected only to make the app work and is stored on NIGOH's own server.
  • We don't show ads or sell your data.
  • Only the linked parent can see the child's location and apps.
  • Location history is kept for 30 days. When you remove a child, their entire history is deleted.

1. Who we are

NIGOH Family is a parental-control app for Android, made in Tajikistan. The app and website connect to NIGOH's own server. We don't use Firebase or any other third-party cloud storage for family data.

What we don't collect

  • Your child's messages in other apps (Telegram, WhatsApp, etc.).
  • Photos, videos, contacts or passwords from your child's phone.
  • Voice call recordings. Calls go directly between the phones and aren't recorded.

3. Why

  • Location — to show where your child is, their 24-hour history and safe-place alerts.
  • Apps and time — limits, blocking, schedules and weekly reports.
  • Chat and notifications — family communication, SOS and requests for more time.
  • Battery and connection status — alerts when the battery is low or the phone goes offline.
  • Website statistics — to understand which pages are useful. We don't share these statistics with anyone.

4. Who can see it

Only the parent who linked the child's phone with a one-time pairing code can see the child's data. Children can see their own rules under “My rules.” The server administrator may have access to the database, but uses it only to fix errors and respond to your request.

We don't sell data or give it to advertisers, and we disclose it only when legally required by the authorities of the Republic of Tajikistan.

5. Third-party services

  • Google, GitHub, Apple — only if you sign in with one of these services. We receive your email and name from them.
  • OpenStreetMap — map images on the parent's phone are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers.
  • Google STUN servers — to establish a voice call connection. Voice does not pass through them.
  • Google Fonts — the website font.

6. How long we keep it

When a parent removes a child, that child's location, chat, safe places, notifications, requests and call history are deleted immediately.

7. Your rights

  • Access — all of your child's data is visible in the parent app.
  • Correction — change the name, photo and rules in settings.
  • Remove a child — remove your child using the parent PIN; their history will be deleted.
  • Delete your account — write to us using the contact form. The account and its data will be deleted within 30 days.
  • Withdraw permissions — you can turn off any permission in Android settings, but some features will stop working.

8. Children

NIGOH Family is made for parents. The child app is installed only with a parent's consent and on a phone they manage. The app isn't hidden: its icon is visible and every permission is explained during setup. We recommend talking to your child before installing it — see our tips for parents.

9. Data protection

All connections are encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords and session tokens are stored in the database only as a hash. Read more on the Security page.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will appear above and the change will be noted under “What's new”. We'll also notify you in the app about important changes.
