Security & privacy

Your family's data — for your family only

Your child's location, apps and messages are personal. On this page we explain in plain terms how we store them and who can see them.

Protection at every step

From the moment you sign in to every map update.

Encrypted connection

All data between the phones and the server travels over HTTPS. It can't be read in transit.

Verified sign-in

Sign-in with Google or email goes directly through the NIGOH server. The server verifies the Google token itself and gives the phone a dedicated key, which is stored in the database only as a hash.

Our own server

Family data is stored on the NIGOH server, which we manage ourselves.

One-time code

The linking code works only once and expires after 15 minutes.

Linked parents only

Only the parent who linked the child's phone can see the child's location and app list.

PIN and uninstall protection

The parent PIN protects settings and removing a child. Without it, the app can't be uninstalled from the child's phone.

What we collect and why

Only what the app needs to work. Every piece of data has a purpose.

DataPurpose
LocationShowing the parent where the child is on a map
App list and iconsSo the parent can see apps and set rules for them
Usage timeCalculating screen time and daily limits
Chat messagesDelivering messages between parent and child in the family chat
Email and name from GoogleSigning in to the account and identifying family members

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't monitor in secret. Your child knows the app is installed.
  • We have no access to messages in other apps, or to your child's passwords and photos.
Transparency

Transparency for your child

NIGOH Family isn't hidden on your child's phone. The app icon is visible, and every permission is explained during setup.

We believe parental control should be built on trust. When children know what is monitored and why, they accept the rules more easily and come to you on their own when something goes wrong.

Talk to your child before installing. Explain that the app is there to keep them safe, not to spy on them. Agree on the screen time rules together.

You're in control

You can unlink at any time or sign out of your account.

Remove a child

Remove a child from the list using the parent PIN. The link between the phones is fully revoked.

Sign out

Sign out in the app or on the website at any time. Signing in again requires new verification.

Delete your account

To delete your account and its data, contact us through your account on the website.
