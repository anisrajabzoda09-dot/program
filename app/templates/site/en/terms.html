1. The service

NIGOH Family consists of the Android app, the nigohfamily.qobus.tj website and the NIGOH server. It lets parents see their child's location, limit screen time, block apps and stay in touch with their child. The app is free and has no ads.

2. Who may use it

  • Only an adult (18+) who is a parent or legal guardian may create a parent account.
  • Install the child app only on a phone that belongs to your minor child or that you manage.

3. Proper use

  • Before installing, tell your child what the app does and why.
  • Set age-appropriate rules and discuss them with your child.
  • Don't share your child's data with strangers.

4. What is prohibited

  • Secretly installing the app on an adult's phone — whether a spouse, relative or employee — without their consent.
  • Using it for stalking, intimidation or any illegal activity.
  • Trying to break into the server, obtain other families' data or overload the server with pointless requests.
  • Modifying and distributing a modified APK under the NIGOH Family name.

If we discover such use, we will block the account without warning.

5. Account and PIN

You are responsible for keeping your password, pairing code and parent PIN private. The pairing code is for one-time use and expires after 15 minutes. If you think someone has accessed your account, change your password and write to us.

6. Limitations

  • The service is provided “as is.” We try to keep it working at all times, but the internet, server or phone may become unavailable.
  • Location depends on GPS and the network, so it may be inaccurate or delayed.
  • A technically skilled child may find ways around some restrictions. The parent will be notified when this happens, but not always.
  • The app doesn't replace parental attention, conversation or trust.

7. Emergencies

The SOS button sends an alert to the parent, but it isn't an emergency service. If there is real danger, immediately call 112 (general emergency), 101 (fire), 102 (police) or 103 (ambulance).

8. Stopping use

You may remove a child, uninstall the app and request account deletion at any time — see the Privacy Policy.

9. Law and changes

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Tajikistan. If the terms change, the new date will appear above. Continued use means that you agree to the new terms. Questions: contact us.
