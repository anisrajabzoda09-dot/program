App control

Every app under your watch

A full list of the apps on your child's phone, with icons and today's usage time. You can set a separate rule for each one.

  • Block or unblock any app with a single switch.
  • Daily limit: 15 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours, 2 hours, 4 hours or no limit.
  • “Homework time”: choose days of the week and hours, for example Monday to Friday, 16:00–18:00. The app is blocked during that time.
  • “Pause”: all apps are blocked at once, for example during dinner or lessons.
  • Screen time at a glance: total usage today, time remaining and the number of blocked apps.
  • New apps your child installs appear in the list automatically with a “New” badge.
  • Extra time: your child sends a request from the app, and you grant +15, +30 or +60 minutes with one tap.
  • “Always allowed”: essential apps, such as the phone or maps, are never blocked.
  • Categories: view games, social, video and education apps separately and block a whole category at once.
  • Weekly report: screen time for the past 7 days and the most-used apps.
Location

Know where your child is

Your child's location is shown on a map. You don't need to call or text them to check.

  • A live map based on OpenStreetMap.
  • The time of the last update is shown next to the map.
  • Location updates automatically, with no action from your child, even indoors.
  • 24-hour history: your child's route is drawn as a line on the map.
  • Safe places: add “Home” or “School” and see where your child is.
  • Your child's phone battery level on their card.
Communication

Family chat built into the app

Parent and child can talk directly in NIGOH Family. No other app needed.

  • Text messages between parent and child, with “Read” receipts.
  • Quick replies for your child: “I've arrived”, “I'm on my way”, “Pick me up”.
  • SOS button: your child holds it, and the parent gets a red alert with their location.
  • Voice calls over the internet between parent and child.
  • Notifications even when the app is closed; SOS comes with a loud alarm.

Protection

Rules keep working, even offline

When your child opens a blocked app, the NIGOH screen covers it. Rules are stored on the phone itself.

  • A block screen on your child's phone in place of the blocked app.
  • Works offline: the rules are saved on the phone.
  • While online, rules sync from the server roughly every 15 seconds.
  • The parent PIN protects settings changes and removing a child.
  • Without the PIN, your child can't uninstall the app from the phone.
  • “Bedtime”: for example, from 21:30 to 07:00 all apps except “Always allowed” ones are blocked.
  • “Study mode”: during school hours, games, social and video apps are blocked automatically; calls, SMS and educational apps stay available.
  • Alerts when the battery is low and when your child's phone goes offline.
  • In “My rules”, your child can see which apps are blocked and how long limits last.
