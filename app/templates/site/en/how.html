How it works

From download to control —
in a few minutes

One app on two phones. When you first open it, you choose a role: Parent or Child. Then the phones are linked with a QR code.

Three steps

Install and link

Install the app on both phones

Get the APK file from the download page. The same file works for the parent's phone and the child's phone. If Android asks, allow your browser to “Install unknown apps”.

Choose “Child” on your child's phone

The permission center shows step by step what needs to be turned on and why. Once everything is ready, a QR code and a 6-digit code appear on the screen.

Choose “Parent” on your phone

Sign in and tap “Add child”. Point the camera at the QR code or enter the 6-digit code manually. Linking takes less than a minute.

Two roles

Each phone has its own job

It's one app, but parent and child see it differently.

On the parent's phone

  • Your child's location on a map and the time of the last update.
  • A list of apps with icons and today's usage time.
  • Blocking, daily limits and “Homework time” for each app.
  • “Pause” to block all apps at once.
  • Chat, SOS alerts and extra time requests.
  • A PIN for important settings.

On the child's phone

  • The app is installed and visible. Your child knows it's there.
  • Location and the app list are sent to the parent.
  • A blocked app is covered by the NIGOH screen.
  • Rules work offline too.
  • Chat with the parent, an SOS button and “My rules”.
  • Can't be uninstalled without the parent PIN.
Permissions

Why each permission is needed

These permissions are granted on your child's phone. We only ask for what the app can't work without.

Location

So the parent can see the child's location on a map. This data is visible only to the linked parent.

Notifications

For chat messages, and so the app can show that it's running in the background.

Camera

Only for scanning the QR code when linking.

Usage access

To know how many minutes each app has been used. Screen time and limits are calculated from this.

Display over other apps

To show the NIGOH screen on top of an app that has been blocked.

Accessibility

To detect which app was just opened, so the rules take effect immediately.

Android 13 and later. If Android says the setting is restricted when you turn on Accessibility, go to Settings → Apps → NIGOH Family, tap ⋮ in the top corner and choose “Allow restricted settings”. Then return to the permission center.
After linking

What happens next

App list

Within a minute, all the apps on your child's phone appear on your phone automatically.

Rules

Block an app or set a limit. Changes reach your child's phone in about 15 seconds.

Offline

Rules are stored on your child's phone and work without the internet too.
