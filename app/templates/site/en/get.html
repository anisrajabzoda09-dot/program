Installation

How to install

The app isn't on Google Play, so Android asks for one extra permission.

Download the file

Tap “Download APK” or scan the QR code.

Allow the installation

Android will ask you to let your browser “Install unknown apps”. Allow it and go back.

Install the app

Open the downloaded file and tap “Install”.

Open it and choose a role

Choose “Parent” on your phone and “Child” on your child's phone.

Android 13 and later. On your child's phone, before turning on Accessibility, you may need to go to: App info → ⋮ → “Allow restricted settings”.

The same APK is installed on both phones. The role — parent or child — is chosen when you first open the app.

After installing

Parent's phone

  • Choose the “Parent” role.
  • Sign in with Google or email.
  • Set a parent PIN in settings.
  • Scan your child's QR code or enter the 6-digit code.

Child's phone

  • Choose the “Child” role.
  • Turn on permissions step by step in the permission center.
  • Show the QR code or the 6-digit code to the parent.
  • The code works once and expires after 15 minutes.
