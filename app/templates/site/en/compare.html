Which one is right for you?

Choose NIGOH Family if

  • you want the app in Tajik;
  • you want chat, calls and SOS in the same app;
  • you don't want to create a Google account for your child;
  • you want to prevent the app from being uninstalled without a parent's permission.

Choose Family Link if

  • your phone is an iPhone;
  • you want to approve every new app before installation;
  • your family already uses Google services (Gmail, Play);
  • installation from Google Play matters to you.
