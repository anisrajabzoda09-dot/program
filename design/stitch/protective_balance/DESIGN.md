## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a respectful, calm, and protective philosophy designed for parental wellbeing and cross-generational trust. Rather than relying on alarming surveillance metaphors, predatory telemetry dashboards, or punitive trackers, the interface champions mutual transparency, collective digital health, and quiet guardianship. 

The emotional tone balances institutional integrity with approachable warmth:
- **Protective & Grounded:** Deep, reassuring structures provide security without authoritarian friction.
- **Privacy-Centric & Transparent:** Visual models make telemetry consent, data access, and family pacts readable at a glance.
- **Calm & Humane:** Reduces digital anxiety using generous breathing room, soft micro-interactions, and restorative tones over high-stress alerts.

### Aesthetic Direction: Modern Protective Minimalism
A clean, refined aesthetic fusing high-clarity modernism with soft, tactile containment. Visual structures leverage broad tonal surfaces, crisp protective strokes, generous negative space, and pill-anchored interactive regions. The aesthetic purposefully avoids sharp militaristic edges, neon alarm colors, and claustrophobic tracking tables, maintaining a serene, family-friendly, and enterprise-grade reliability.

## Colors

The color system is calibrated to signal stability, clarity, and safety while prioritizing cognitive comfort across daylight and evening modes.

### Palette Architecture
- **Deep Navy (`#102A43`):** Primary identity anchor; conveys authority, safety, and foundational architecture.
- **Action Blue (`#1463FF` / Dark: `#5B8FFF`):** Directs intentional, affirmative interactions and forward flow.
- **Trust Teal (`#00A896` / Dark: `#35D0BA`):** Signifies healthy states, consent-backed permissions, and active protection.
- **Primary Text (`#243B53` / Dark: `#F4F7FA`):** High-legibility ink balanced for optical softness against backgrounds.
- **Secondary Text (`#627D98` / Dark: `#B8C7D9`):** Subdued contextual guidance and supportive metadata.
- **Surfaces & Borders:** Light mode defaults to a restorative backdrop (`#F5F7FA`), accentuating structural cards with Light Blue Surface (`#E9F1FF`) and hair-fine framing borders (`#D9E2EC`).

### Functional Feedback Tokens
- **Success (`#16803A`):** Safe states, agreed screen schedules, completed pacts.
- **Warning (`#F79009`):** Usage limit approach, device battery warnings, unconfirmed consent changes.
- **Danger (`#D92D20`):** Critical perimeter bypasses, urgent SOS actions, or immediate privacy revocations.

### Dark Mode Adjustments
Dark mode converts surfaces from `#081522` (Canvas) to `#102A43` (Containers) and `#173B5E` (Elevated Cards). High-frequency accent blues and teals step down luminance to preserve nighttime circadian rhythms.

## Typography

Typography centers on pure readability, optical clarity, and democratic accessibility. The system relies on **Inter** as the foundational standard while embracing system-level typography fallbacks (`-apple-system`, `SF Pro`, `Roboto`, `Segoe UI`, `Ubuntu Sans`) for zero layout shifts and low overhead on mobile devices.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Reserved for daily overview summaries, onboarding milestone states, and digital balance indexes. Tight negative tracking keeps larger scales cohesive.
- **Section Headlines:** Distinct medium-bold styling ensures rapid visual parsing during active family routines.
- **Body Text:** Generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensure long privacy disclosures, device access pacts, and context explanations remain readable across varied viewing distances.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Used for privacy status chips, mutual verification tags, and device battery/connectivity chips. Uses slightly loose tracking to preserve legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid, responsive structure predicated on an 8-point geometric scale, shifting to a 4-point scale for fine-grained internal component spacing.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` outer canvas margin, `1rem` column gutters. Components stack into single-column flows prioritizing one-handed thumb-zone reach for essential controls.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` outer canvas margin, `1.5rem` gutters. Splits family member activity and status dashboards into side-by-side balanced cards.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a max-width of `1280px` with `3rem` margins. Introduces persistent side navigation panels, central family wellbeing canvas, and contextual inspection rails.

### Spacing Philosophy
Negative space is treated as an active calming agent. Crowded screens induce anxiety; this system intentionally pairs dense metrics with generous section offsets (`space-xl`), grouping related settings inside clean visual containers to prevent visual exhaustion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through architectural tonal layers and soft, diffused atmospheric shadows rather than aggressive z-axis elevation. This communicates privacy and stability without visual noise.

### Layering Model
1. **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Background foundation (`#F5F7FA` light, `#081522` dark).
2. **Structural Surfaces (Level 1):** Main family cards, dashboard segments, and feed items rendered in crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) or `#102A43` in dark mode, enclosed by low-contrast outlines (`#D9E2EC` / `#173B5E`).
3. **Elevated Overlays & Flyouts (Level 2):** Quick adjustments, limit configuration sheets, and member status modals. Elevated with a deeply diffused, tinted shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.08)`.
4. **Floating Action Sheets & Alerts (Level 3):** Reserved exclusively for time extension agreements, transparent screen limit reminders, and safety dialogs: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.16)`.

### Border Integrity
Every card and container utilizes a 1px border. Shadow is never applied without a supporting border boundary, anchoring shapes even under high ambient light or display glare.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape language, projecting modern, safe, and family-friendly approachability without appearing childlike or undisciplined.

### Radius Distribution
- **Small Controls (`rounded-sm` / 0.25rem):** Internal micro-badges, status dots, and fine inline indicators.
- **Interactive Form Elements (`rounded` / 0.5rem):** Standard input fields, menu dropdowns, table cells, and segment containers.
- **Cards & Content Modules (`rounded-lg` / 1rem):** Primary feature modules, family member wellbeing summaries, and device usage breakdowns.
- **Modals & Dialogs (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem):** Time-limit pact flows, consent negotiation sheets, and primary surface sheets.
- **Pill Shapes (`rounded-full`):** Filter tags, interactive quick-action buttons, presence indicators, and user avatars.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid Action Blue (`#1463FF`), white typography, `rounded-full` or `rounded` (0.5rem) based on density. Zero flat dropshadow; micro-scale press transformation (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Light Blue Surface (`#E9F1FF`) with Primary Text (`#243B53`) and crisp border.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, Action Blue or Deep Navy text, subtle slate hover state.
- **Mutual Agreement Action:** Trust Teal (`#00A896`) background, white text, indicating shared multi-party authorization (e.g., both parent and teen agree to limit adjustment).

### Cards & Modules
- **Wellbeing Cards:** Solid white surfaces, `rounded-lg` (1rem), 1px border (`#D9E2EC`). Never uses heavy dropshadows. Includes top-right mutual consent badges to clearly display whether telemetry is active or locked.
- **Member Overview Cards:** Highlighted with subtle accent indicators; avatar with Trust Teal presence dot, device health icon, and transparent, aggregated screen balance bar (never raw keystrokes or invasive private data feeds).

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Outlined 1px `#D9E2EC`, surface white, `rounded` (0.5rem), interior padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Focus state features a crisp Action Blue outline (`#1463FF`) with a soft 3px outer glow (`rgba(20, 99, 255, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `0.5rem` radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Trust Teal fill on active check state to indicate verified protective parameters.

### Chips & Tags
- **Privacy Status Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, label-sm font. Trust Teal tint for "Shared Transparently," Deep Navy tint for "Offline Safeguard," and Warning Orange tint for "Pact Expiring Soon."

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Aggregated Activity Row:** Displays category summaries (e.g., "Education & Research - 1h 45m") instead of detailed surveillance spy logs. Uses clean line dividers (`#D9E2EC`), generous vertical rhythm (`space-md`), and clear interactive toggles.

### Specialized Family Wellbeing Components
- **The Digital Pact Drawer:** Slide-over modal allowing parent and child to co-sign device schedules, displaying shared agreements with dual confirmation controls.
- **Curfew & Downtime Dial:** Circular soft-slider indicating family wind-down time, transitioning seamlessly between daylight blues and evening twilight tones.
